Project 01Sample · fictional data

Monthly Bank Reconciliation

Harbour Lane Cafe Ltd · Xero · 31 March

Reconcile the business current account to the bank statement, clear unmatched items, and confirm the closing balance ties to the general ledger.

Approach

  • Match bank feed lines to bills, invoices and bank rules.
  • Code unmatched items and query anything unclear with the owner.
  • Identify outstanding cheques and deposits in transit.
Result

Bank and ledger tie to a zero difference, with every exception documented for the owner.
Project 02Sample · fictional data

Irish VAT3 Return Preparation

Brennan Trade Supplies Ltd · QuickBooks Online, Dext · Jan to Feb

Prepare the bi-monthly VAT3 return from clean ledger data, review VAT codes, and confirm the amount payable before filing through Revenue Online Service (ROS).

Approach

  • Verify invoices were captured in Dext and published to QuickBooks with correct VAT codes.
  • Run the VAT report and check sales at 23% and 13.5% against invoice listings.
  • Review non-allowable VAT items, then prepare the figures for approval.
Result

Return prepared with a supporting workpaper and reviewed before filing to meet the Revenue deadline.
Project 03Sample · fictional data

Fixed Asset Register & SOP

Northgate Logistics Group · Multi-country · Xero

Maintain the fixed asset register for a multi-country entity, processing additions, disposals, transfers and trade-ins with approvals and audit-ready support.

Controls applied

  • Approval and supporting documents attached before posting.
  • Depreciation starts in the month of capitalization; gain or loss calculated on every disposal.
  • Register reconciled to the general ledger every month-end.
Result

A clear, repeatable procedure that new team members can follow with less hand-holding.
Project 04Sample · fictional data

Aged Payables Review & Month-End Close

Kestrel Print Services Ltd · QuickBooks Online · 30 June

Review supplier balances by age, prioritize payments to protect supplier relationships and cash, then complete the month-end close.

Approach

  • Age every supplier balance and flag overdue items.
  • Prioritize the payment run by age and supplier importance.
  • Work through the month-end checklist and send the management pack.
Result

A clean close and a clear payment priority list, so the owner knows who to pay first and why.
